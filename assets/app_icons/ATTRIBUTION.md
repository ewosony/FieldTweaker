# Branded logo assets

The prepackaged vector-style marks for Chrome, Firefox, Brave, Discord, Telegram, Steam, Git, and Spotify were generated from **Font Awesome Free 6** brand SVG icons (https://fontawesome.com/license/free), licensed under **CC BY 4.0** for the icons: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . We recolored and rasterized the marks into PNG files to fit the UI.

Other branded logos may be downloaded **on the user’s PC** from each publisher’s web assets, Simple Icons (CC0 project artwork; individual trademarks belong to their owners), GitHub organization avatars, or a favicon as last resort. See `personal_lite/brand_icons.py` for source URLs. Trademarks remain owned by their respective rights holders; FIELD is not affiliated with these publishers.
