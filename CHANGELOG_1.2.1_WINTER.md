# FIELD 1.2.1 — Winter Edition

- Optional seasonal palette for light and dark UI.
- Animated snowfall and gently glowing garlands painted by a transparent Qt overlay.
- Festive banner and badge on dashboard/sidebar.
- Instantly disable or restore decorations without refreshing app pages; preference remembered.
- No new remote assets, dependencies, registry edits or background services.
- FIELD 1.2 app installer, app icons, cleanup, power plans, tweaks and activity journal retained.

## Compatibility
Python 3.11 / PySide6 6.7+, Windows 10/11.
The holiday overlay is non-interactive and does not block navigation or app installation.
